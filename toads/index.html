How the weather station works

  1. ESP32sensors
  2. MQTTMosquitto
  3. Telegrafingest
  4. InfluxDBstorage
  5. This pagevia Cloudflare

An ESP32 reads three sensors every 5 minutes (DS18B20 in the soil, a capacitive moisture probe, and a BME280 for air temperature, humidity and pressure) and publishes the readings over MQTT to a home server.
